# Gadget Cover insurance policy (demo)

## What Is Covered

### 1. Theft
If your insured device is stolen, you may be eligible for compensation.

**What's required:** a police report and proof of purchase.

**Payout:** the purchase value of the device, capped at €1,000, less a €50 excess.

### 2. Accidental Damage
If your insured device is accidentally damaged, you may be eligible for the cost of repair.

**What's required:** a repair quote and proof of purchase.

**Payout:** the repair cost, never more than the purchase value, less a €50 excess.

## What Is Not Covered
- Incidents outside the policy period
- Claims reported more than 48 hours after the incident
- Devices left unattended in a public place
